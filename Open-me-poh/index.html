THE WEIGHT OF UNSPOKEN THINGS

If this page is a piece of my heart, then this photo belongs here because it’s one you love.

To the one who inspired these words,

You are like lilies—soft, graceful, and effortlessly beautiful in a way that doesn’t need attention, but somehow always get it anyway.

You’re the kind of person who doesn’t need to try too hard to stand out, you naturally do, just by being yourself. Like lilies blooming

gently in a quiet garden, you have this calm presence that makes everything feel lighter and more peaceful when you’re around. But

at the same time, you’re not just soft and calm. Sometimes, you are like lilies that somehow got a playful breeze passing through—

unexpected, funny, and full of little moments that make people smile without even realizing it. One moment you’re composed, and next,

saying or doing someting that makes me think, ”Uhm, so this is your chaotic side.” And honestly? It makes you even more endearing.

There’s something about you that feels delicate, like petals that could be easily overlooked—but the more you pay attention, the more

you realize how beautfil every detail is. The way you talk, the way you react to things, even the way you get attached to little things you

care about...it all feels soft in the same way lilies are soft. And the more I get to know you, the more realize that I don’t just admire you...

I find myself looking forward to you, your presence, and even the smallest moments with you. It’s something I didn’t expect, but it slowly

became real. So if I were to put all these feelings into the simplest words...

I like you, Susej Ebero Rosario.
